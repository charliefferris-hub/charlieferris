# Charlie Ferris – brand guidelines (V2)

Source of truth for the look of charlieferris.vercel.app. Agreed 23 Oct 2026. Design canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/Fv4f8cAACXqUby67z12c8F (boards "V2 · Cobalt" and "V2 full palette").

## The idea
- Service, made human.
- Bold, bright and friendly, like a brand that enjoys looking after people.
- Confident enough for employers, playful enough to remember.
- Inspiration: UW (uw.co.uk) for vibrancy, colour drench, white cards with colour pops, two shades of each colour, chunky illustrations.

## Personality
- Warm, never twee.
- Confident, never boastful.
- Playful in the details, serious about the work.

## Voice
- First person, short sentences, plain British English.
- Real numbers over claims ("400 people", not "large teams").
- Bold the one phrase that matters in each paragraph.
- Short, playful button labels ("Take off", "Get help"), never "Click here".
- One light joke per page at most.

## Colour rules
- Cobalt main is the drench at the top of the page and the footer.
- Cobalt light (ice blue) partners cobalt: key words on cobalt, one feature band per page.
- Cream is the canvas for everything between the top and the footer.
- White is for cards only.
- Every button is marigold main with ink text and an ink outline.
- Coral and lime are pops: card shadows, icon chips, illustrations. Never text or large areas.
- Ink is for text and outlines. Never pure black elsewhere.

## Three-shade rule
- Light: icon chips and soft panels.
- Main: solid card shadows and colour blocks.
- Deep: hover states and dark accents.
- One colour family per card (coral card = coral chip + coral shadow).

## Type
- Headlines: Bricolage Grotesque, weight 800, big and tight (letter-spacing about -0.02em).
- Body and buttons: DM Sans (400, 500, 700).
- Two fonts only. No handwriting, no serif.

## Shapes
- Outlines: 2px ink on cards and buttons, 3px on large blocks.
- Corners: 16px cards, 8px buttons, 10px icon chips.
- Shadows: solid, offset 8px down and right, in the card's main colour. Never blurred.

## Layout
- Cobalt drench at the top, cream middle, cobalt footer.
- Centred, generous spacing, max four cards in a row, content width about 1180px.
- Every page works on a phone.

## Illustration style
- Chunky, flat drawings of everyday objects in the spirit of UW: mugs, headsets, planes, speech bubbles, hands.
- Objects float just above a small flat ink oval shadow.
- Bursts of energy welcome: motion lines, sparkles, steam, confetti.
- Slight hand-drawn wobble.
- Hands are fine; avoid detailed faces.

## Illustration colour
- Outlines: ink #101010, always the same thickness.
- Fills: main shades of cobalt, marigold, coral, lime, plus cream and white.
- Shading: deep shades only, flat blocks.
- Highlights: light shades.
- Up to three colours per drawing, plus ink and cream.
- No gradients, textures or photographic effects.
- Never place a drawing on a background of its own main colour.

## Illustration subjects
- Each links to Charlie's story: headset (leadership), plane (flight tracker), speech bubble (help centre), sparkle or laptop (AI).

## Avoid
- Pastel or greyed-down colours (this is why earlier mist-blue options looked like a nursery).
- Blurred drop shadows, gradients.
- Stock photos, emoji.
- More than one pop colour per card.
